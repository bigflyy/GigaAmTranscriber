Speaker label models (downloaded on request, not bundled)

The app downloads these files from the GigaAM Transcriber "models-1" release
(assets speakers_community1.*). They were made by
experiments/diarization/export_speaker_models.py from the models below.

   Changes: converted from PyTorch to ONNX; weights unchanged.

2. WeSpeaker ResNet34-LM, VoxCeleb (embedding.onnx)
   https://huggingface.co/pyannote/wespeaker-voxceleb-resnet34-LM
   (a wrapper of https://github.com/wenet-e2e/wespeaker)
   Authors: Hongji Wang, Chengdong Liang, Shuai Wang, Zhengyang Chen, Binbin Zhang,
   Xu Xiang, Yanlei Deng, Yanmin Qian (WeSpeaker, ICASSP 2023); pyannote wrapper by
   Herve Bredin.
   License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
   Changes: converted from PyTorch to ONNX together with pyannote's weighted
   statistics pooling, so one run gives the voice print of each speaker of a
   window; weights unchanged.

3. pyannote/speaker-diarization-community-1, PLDA (speakers.npz)
   https://huggingface.co/pyannote/speaker-diarization-community-1
   Authors: pyannoteAI and the pyannote.audio contributors.
   License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
   Changes: the PLDA's generalised eigen-decomposition (as in pyannote.audio's
   vbx_setup) is stored precomputed, with the powerset table of segmentation-3.0.
   speakers.json holds the pipeline's default settings (threshold 0.6, Fa 0.07,
   Fb 0.8).

The clustering code that uses them (gigaam_app/diarize.py) is a port of
pyannote.audio 4 (MIT, https://github.com/pyannote/pyannote-audio).
